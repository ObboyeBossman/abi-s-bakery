Our Roots & Heritage

The Story Behind Abi's Bakery

Abi's Bakery began in 2019 in Abigail Amoah's kitchen in Ghana. Rooted in cherished Ghanaian baking traditions, Abigail set out to elevate beloved local favorites—from crunchy meat pies and fluffy bofrot to classic rock buns and pillowy butter bread.

What started as weekend orders for family and close neighbors quickly grew into a beloved local bakehouse. Abigail ensures every batch is made from scratch with pure butter, fresh spices, and zero commercial compromises.
